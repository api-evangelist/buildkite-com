You can now subscribe to RSS feeds for macOS base image releases on Buildkite hosted agents, making it easier to keep up with changes to your build environments. For updates to stable base images used in production, add the stable RSS feed to your preferred RSS reader. If your organisation wants access to the latest Xcode betas and runtimes, request a move to the canary channel.
