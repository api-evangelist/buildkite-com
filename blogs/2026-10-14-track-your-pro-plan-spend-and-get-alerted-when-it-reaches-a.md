If your organization is on a monthly Pro plan, you can now see what your next invoice is shaping up to be before the billing period ends. Set a spend alert to get an email when the estimate reaches an amount you choose, so a busy month of builds doesn't become a surprise invoice. The new Usage & spend tab in Settings > Billing shows your estimated spend and the days left in the billing period, followed by a line-by-line estimate of your next invoice.
