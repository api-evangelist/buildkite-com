Your test suite's Summary page now brings together more of the data you need to keep your suite healthy and fast, so you can see where time is going and where reliability is slipping without digging through individual tests. To find what's slowing your suite down, Slowest tests lists the 10 slowest tests in the selected period, and the suite and test duration distributions show how execution time is spread across runs and tests, with percentile markers. Values above p95 are omitted to keep the distributions readable.
